CFM per ton

This app will calculate the CFM per ton of the blower speed used for the data entered. Before using this app, please ensure that the manifold gas pressure is correct. Also that the filter is clean.

1.08 is the specific heat of air (.24 btu) X the specific density of air (.075 lbs ft³) X the number of minutes per hour (to convert it to per minute). At sea level. This is the most common used number in these calculations.
